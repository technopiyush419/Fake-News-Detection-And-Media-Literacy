⚠️ Warning Signs of Fake News

🚨 Sensational Headlines

Headlines designed mainly to shock or scare readers.

❗ Emotional Language

Content that strongly tries to make you angry, scared or excited.

🔗 Unknown Source

The website or author is difficult to identify.

📸 Misleading Images

Images may be used without proper context.

📢 Pressure to Share

Messages asking you to immediately share or forward information.

❓ No Evidence

Claims are made without reliable evidence or supporting sources.

ℹ️ About This Project

Fake News Detection & Media Literacy is an educational web application designed to create awareness about misinformation and responsible digital media usage.

🎯 Project Objectives

  • Identify common warning signs of fake news.
  • Improve digital media awareness.
  • Encourage users to verify information.
  • Promote responsible online sharing.
⚠️ Important: This application uses simple rule-based text analysis. It cannot guarantee whether a news article is actually true or false.
